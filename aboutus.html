About us

Good food, honest prices

A small shop with a big menu, built by students who love to eat.

Our story

Online FoodShop began as a college mini project: a simple idea to make ordering a quick meal as easy as picking from a menu. We cook fast food, drinks and desserts fresh to order and keep prices friendly for students.

Every dish is marked vegetarian or non-vegetarian, and you can save favorites, apply coupons and track your orders from one place.
