About Us


Founded in 2009, Menufy offers mobile and online meal ordering capabilities to restaurants in thousands of cities in the US. Menufy provides digital menus on websites and in its app, from which customers can securely pay with the most options available out of all restaurant e-commerce systems.

Community caring & giving

Menufy supports:


  • Harvesters, a food bank that serves people in need at food pantries, community kitchens, senior centers, and homeless shelters in 26 counties across Northeastern Kansas and Northwestern Missouri. Learn more and support the cause at harvesters.org.
  • After The Harvest, a non-profit organization that rescues nutritious fruits and vegetables from going to waste and donates them to food banks and feeding agencies that primarily serve the greater Kansas City area. Learn more and support the cause at aftertheharvestkc.org.
  • Sheffield Place, a Kansas City-based non-profit organization that transitions mothers and children from homelessness to self-sufficiency. Learn more and support the cause at sheffieldplace.org.
